Bird netting is an effective method used to keep birds away from your balcony, terrace, windows, and premises. It protects your property from unwanted mess, droppings, and severe diseases like rabies, Lyme disease, encephalitis, Paratyphoid, and Tuberculosis caused by bird droppings and pests. It also prevents gutters and drains from getting clogged.
Bird netting is important because it wards off birds without harming or killing them, protecting your health against infectious airborne microbes and parasites. It also maintains the cleanliness and shine of your building walls and floors, preventing acidic pigeon droppings from corroding railings and paint.
